The Core Idea Behind Kalakriti

As an engineer, I spend a lot of time building structured systems. But I've always believed that technology is at its best when it's infused with art. Kalakriti is a personal passion project where my code meets my canvas.

All the artworks featured here are my own creations. I built this visual gallery not just to show my hobby, but to showcase the design sensibilities and creative instincts that I bring to my engineering work. Thank you for stepping into my world of colors and code!
